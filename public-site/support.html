Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open the app, go to either Settings or the Arcade Shop > Crystals tab, and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID on your device.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your cloud account directly inside the app: go to Settings and tap the delete icon (trash icon). This instantly deletes your profile, weekly leaderboard rank, and purchase history from our servers.

How does TOYBOX mode keep children safe?

TOYBOX mode is designed for young players (ages 4–7). In this mode, leaderboards and external links are disabled, and all purchases and mode changes require passing an adult math gate.

What should I do if a purchase was charged but items didn't appear?

First, make sure you have an active internet connection and tap RESTORE PURCHASES in the Shop. If your items still don't appear, forward your Apple Store receipt to support@zmz.ca and we will assist you.
