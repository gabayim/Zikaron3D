Privacy Policy for Zikaron3D

Effective Date: November 2026

Zikaron3D ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our mobile application Zikaron3D.

Core Privacy Principles:
  • No third-party advertisements or tracking SDKs.
  • No personal data (names, email addresses, phone numbers) is requested or required to play.
  • Zero tracking across other apps and websites.

1. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Zikaron3D is designed for families and players of all ages, featuring a dedicated TOYBOX mode for young children (ages 4–7).

  • Data Minimization: We do not collect personal identifiers from children. In TOYBOX mode, social features and leaderboards are disabled, and all purchases and external links are locked behind a parental gate.
  • Parental Gate: Any attempt to access in-app purchases, external web links, or change modes requires solving an adult math challenge.
  • No Behavioral Advertising: We never show targeted or contextual advertisements to children.

2. Information We Collect and Why

We collect only the minimum data necessary for core game functionality:

  • Anonymous Identifiers: If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an anonymous user ID. We never request your name or email address.
  • Gameplay Progress: Your level completions, high scores, unlocked worlds, and cosmetic items are stored locally and synced to Supabase database so you do not lose progress across reinstalls.
  • Purchases History: When you purchase crystals or streak shields via Apple In-App Purchase, transactions are verified securely via RevenueCat and recorded in your private ledger to ensure items can be restored.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers that adhere to strict privacy and security standards:

  • Apple StoreKit / Sign in with Apple: Handles secure authentication and in-app purchase payments.
  • RevenueCat: Validates store receipts and manages receipt restoration without tracking user identity.
  • Supabase: Provides encrypted cloud database hosting with Row-Level Security (RLS).

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings in the app and tap the delete icon. Confirming deletion immediately wipes your cloud profile, scores, and purchase ledger, and revokes your Apple sign-in credentials.
  • Manual Request: You may also email support@zmz.ca to request complete deletion of any cloud records.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us at: support@zmz.ca.
